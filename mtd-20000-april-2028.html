MTD from April 2028: the £20,000 threshold

The third and, for now, final stage of Making Tax Digital for Income Tax: if your qualifying income in the 2026–27 tax year is over £20,000, you start on 6 April 2028 (GOV.UK).

Which income?

The year that decides it is 6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027, the tax year running now. Qualifying income is self-employment turnover plus property income, before expenses; for jointly owned property, your share only (GOV.UK).

Example: a landlord with £21,500 of rent in 2026–27 and no other business income is over £20,000 and starts on 6 April 2028. With £20,000 exactly, they would not.

£20,000 or less?

You're exempt while your qualifying income stays at £20,000 or less (GOV.UK). Check again each year.

Your first deadlines (2028–29)

Quarterly updates by 7 August 2028, 7 November 2028, 7 February 2029 and 7 May 2029, then the tax return by 31 January 2030.

Why start now

Your 2026–27 records are the ones HMRC will look at. Keeping them digitally from today means the totals are ready, and the switch in 2028 is just a change of routine.
