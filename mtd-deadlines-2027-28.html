MTD quarterly update deadlines for 2027–28

If you join Making Tax Digital for Income Tax on 6 April 2027 (qualifying income over £30,000 in 2025–26), these are your first-year deadlines (HMRC):

UpdateCovers (standard quarters, year to date)Send by
Quarter 16 Apr 2027 – 5 Jul 20277 August 2027
Quarter 26 Apr 2027 – 5 Oct 20277 November 2027
Quarter 36 Apr 2027 – 5 Jan 20287 February 2028
Quarter 46 Apr 2027 – 5 Apr 20287 May 2028
Tax returnWhole year 2027–2831 January 2029

Good to know

  • Each update is a running total from 6 April, so you can correct an earlier quarter in the next one.
  • You send an update for each self-employment and for your UK property business, even if nothing happened that quarter.
  • If you elect calendar quarters (ending 30 June, 30 September, 31 December, 31 March), the deadlines stay the same.
  • Your 2026–27 tax return is still the old way, by 31 January 2028.
