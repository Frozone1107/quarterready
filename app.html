Send it to HMRC

QuarterReady keeps your records and works out the totals. To send a quarterly update you need HMRC-recognised bridging software that accepts a spreadsheet or CSV file. Export the summary, open it in Excel or Google Sheets if needed, and link or import it in your bridging software. Find software on GOV.UK.
