No journeys yet. Each one adds the HMRC flat-rate allowance to "Car, van and travel expenses" in your quarterly summary.

Flat rates can't be used for a vehicle you've claimed capital allowances on, and once you use them for a vehicle you must keep doing so. Parking, tolls and train fares are claimed separately as records. Source: GOV.UK simplified expenses.

Send it to HMRC

QuarterReady keeps your records and works out the totals. To send a quarterly update you need HMRC-recognised bridging software that accepts a spreadsheet or CSV file. Find software on GOV.UK.
