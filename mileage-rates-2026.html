HMRC mileage rates for 2026–27: now 55p a mile

From 6 April 2026, the flat mileage rate for cars and vans rose from 45p to 55p a mile for the first 10,000 business miles. It applies to the self-employed using simplified expenses, and to employers' approved mileage payments (GOV.UK, published 17 June 2026).

Vehicle2026–27Before 6 April 2026
Car or van, first 10,000 business miles55p45p
Car or van, after 10,000 miles25p25p
Motorcycle24p24p

Source: GOV.UK simplified expenses: vehicles.

Example

A sole trader drives 12,000 business miles in 2026–27: 10,000 × 55p = £5,500, plus 2,000 × 25p = £500. Total allowance: £6,000.

The rules

  • You can't use flat rates for a vehicle you've claimed capital allowances on.
  • Once you use flat rates for a vehicle, you keep using them for as long as it's used in the business.
  • Parking, tolls and train fares are claimed on top.
  • Keep a record of each journey: date, where, why, and miles.
