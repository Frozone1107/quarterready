MTD penalties: late submission, late filing and the first year

Short answer: Making Tax Digital for Income Tax (MTD ITSA) uses penalty points. Each late quarterly update or late tax return earns one point; at 4 points you pay £200, and £200 for each further late submission. In the 2026–27 tax year there are no penalties for late quarterly updates (GOV.UK).

Late submission penalty (quarterly updates and tax return)

  • One point per missed deadline, whether it's a quarterly update or the tax return. Only one point per deadline.
  • At 4 points: a £200 penalty, then £200 for every further missed deadline.
  • A single point expires after 24 months. Once you reach the threshold, points are cleared by meeting your deadlines for a period instead.
  • MTD penalty points are separate from VAT penalty points.

MTD penalties in the first year

For the 2026–27 tax year, HMRC doesn't give penalty points for late quarterly updates. You still need to keep digital records and send all four updates before your tax return (GOV.UK). If you start in April 2027, the points apply to your quarterly updates from your first year.

For late payment, your first year gives you 30 days before penalties start instead of 15.

Volunteers

If you've signed up voluntarily, missed quarterly updates don't get penalties; for late tax returns the threshold is 2 points instead of 4.

How to avoid MTD penalties

  • Put 7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May in your calendar (QuarterReady gives you a calendar file with reminders).
  • Record as you go, so each update is just copying totals.
  • Send an update up to 10 days before the period ends if nothing else will come in (GOV.UK).

Common questions

What is the MTD late filing penalty?

For the tax return and each quarterly update, a late submission earns one penalty point. The £200 penalty only starts once you reach 4 points.

Are there MTD penalties in the first year?

For 2026–27 there are no penalties for late quarterly updates. Late tax returns and late payments can still be penalised, with a 30-day grace period for payments in your first year.

When do MTD ITSA penalties start for quarterly updates?

From the 2027–28 tax year, so for the first update due 7 August 2027.

Can HMRC cancel a penalty?

HMRC may cancel penalties or points in exceptional circumstances. You can also appeal if you had a reasonable excuse.
