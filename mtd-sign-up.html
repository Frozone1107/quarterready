How to sign up (register) for Making Tax Digital for Income Tax

Short answer: you register for MTD for Income Tax online with HMRC, using the same user ID and password as your Self Assessment. It's free and takes a few minutes once you have your business details and compatible software. An accountant can register you instead (GOV.UK).

Before you register, have ready

  • Your Self Assessment sign-in details. You must be registered for Self Assessment and have sent a return in the last 2 years.
  • Possibly proof of identity: a passport or driving licence, checked with a phone app.
  • For self-employment: your business name (as on invoices), address and trade.
  • The start date of any business or letting started in the last 2 tax years, and any that has stopped.
  • Compatible software, or a spreadsheet plus bridging software.

How to sign up, step by step

  1. Check you aren't exempt.
  2. Choose your software and check it does what you need.
  3. Sign in to HMRC's sign-up service with your Self Assessment details.
  4. Complete the identity check if asked.
  5. Check each self-employment and property income source, and add any missing.
  6. Choose the tax year you'll start.

The official sign-up service is linked from GOV.UK (GOV.UK).

When to register

Over £30,000 in 2025–266 April 2027 (you can volunteer now for 2027–28)
Over £20,000 in 2026–276 April 2028

If you volunteer for a year already under way, you'll need to send the quarterly updates you missed.

Common questions

Is MTD registration the same as signing up?

Yes. HMRC calls it signing up; it's the same thing as registering for Making Tax Digital for Income Tax. You stay registered for Self Assessment.

Is it free to sign up for MTD?

Yes, HMRC's sign-up service is free. Software can be free or paid.

Do I need software before I sign up?

HMRC asks you to check you have compatible software first, because you'll need it to send your quarterly updates.

Can my accountant sign me up?

Yes. An agent can sign you up through their own HMRC service.
