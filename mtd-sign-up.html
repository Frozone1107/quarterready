How to sign up for Making Tax Digital for Income Tax

Signing up is done online with the same user ID and password you use for Self Assessment (GOV.UK). An agent can sign you up instead.

Before you start, have ready

  • Your Self Assessment sign-in details. You must be registered for Self Assessment and have sent a return in the last 2 years.
  • Possibly proof of identity: a passport or driving licence, checked with a phone app.
  • For self-employment: your business name (as on invoices), address and trade.
  • The start date of any business or letting started in the last 2 tax years, and any that has stopped.
  • Compatible software, or a spreadsheet plus bridging software.

The steps

  1. Check you aren't exempt.
  2. Sign in to HMRC's sign-up service.
  3. Complete the identity check if asked.
  4. Check each self-employment and property income source, and add missing ones.
  5. Choose the tax year you'll start.

The official service and step-by-step guide are on GOV.UK (GOV.UK).

When to sign up

  • Over £50,000 in 2024–25: you should already be using MTD; sign up now. HMRC began signing up people who hadn't from September 2026.
  • Over £30,000 in 2025–26: before 6 April 2027. You can volunteer now for 2027–28.
  • Over £20,000 in 2026–27: before 6 April 2028.

If you volunteer for a year already under way, you'll need to send any quarterly updates you missed.
