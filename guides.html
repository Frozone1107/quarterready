MTD from April 2027: are you in the £30,000 group?

If your qualifying income for the 2025–26 tax year was over £30,000, you need to use Making Tax Digital for Income Tax from 6 April 2027 (GOV.UK).

Qualifying income means your self-employment turnover plus your property income, before expenses. If you own a property jointly, count only your share. Wages, pensions, dividends and partnership profit shares don't count (GOV.UK).

Example: £18,000 of freelance income and £14,000 of rent in 2025–26 gives £32,000. That's over £30,000, so MTD starts on 6 April 2027.

What changes on 6 April 2027

  • You keep digital records of income and expenses.
  • You send a quarterly update by 7 August 2027, 7 November 2027, 7 February 2028 and 7 May 2028 (HMRC).
  • You finalise the year and send your tax return from your software by 31 January 2029.

Can I use a spreadsheet for Making Tax Digital?

Yes. HMRC says bridging software may suit you if you want to keep your digital records in a spreadsheet (GOV.UK: choose the right software). The spreadsheet holds your records; the bridging software reads the totals and sends them to HMRC.

What the spreadsheet needs

  • One row per transaction, with a date, a category and an amount.
  • Categories that match what HMRC asks for in a quarterly update, for example "Rent, rates, insurance and ground rents" or "Car, van and travel expenses".
  • Year-to-date totals per category for each quarter, which the bridging software links to.
  • Mortgage interest on residential lets kept separate from other property expenses.
