MTD quarterly update deadlines for 2026–27

If your qualifying income was over £50,000 in 2024–25, Making Tax Digital for Income Tax started for you on 6 April 2026. These are your first-year deadlines (GOV.UK):

UpdateCovers (standard quarters, year to date)Send by
Quarter 16 Apr 2026 – 5 Jul 20267 August 2026 (passed)
Quarter 26 Apr 2026 – 5 Oct 20267 November 2026
Quarter 36 Apr 2026 – 5 Jan 20277 February 2027
Quarter 46 Apr 2026 – 5 Apr 20277 May 2027
Tax return (final declaration)Whole year 2026–2731 January 2028

With calendar quarters (ending 30 June, 30 September, 31 December and 31 March) the deadlines are the same.

Missed 7 August 2026?

Send the update as soon as you can. HMRC won't give penalty points for late quarterly updates in 2026–27, the first year (GOV.UK). Because each update is a running total from 6 April, your 7 November update also covers the first quarter. The tax return and the tax payment still have their normal penalties. More in MTD penalties.

Good to know

  • A quarterly update isn't a tax bill. Tax is still paid by 31 January (and 31 July if you make payments on account).
  • You send one update for each self-employment and one for your UK property business, even for a quarter with no income.
  • You can correct a figure in the next update. You don't resubmit the old one.
  • Joined from April 2027 instead? See the 2027–28 deadlines.
