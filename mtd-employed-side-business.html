Employed with a side business: do you need MTD?

Your salary doesn't count. For Making Tax Digital for Income Tax, only self-employment turnover and property income count towards the threshold. Employment (PAYE), pensions, dividends and partnership profit shares are left out (GOV.UK).

Examples for 2025–26 (decides April 2027)

SituationQualifying incomeMTD from April 2027?

£38,000 salary + £22,000 weekend photography + £10,000 rent£32,000Yes

It's turnover, not profit

The test uses income before expenses. A side business with £31,000 of sales and £20,000 of costs is over the £30,000 threshold, even though the profit is £11,000.

If you're in

You keep digital records for the side business only and send quarterly updates for it. Your salary still goes on your tax return at year end, as now.
