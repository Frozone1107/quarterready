MTD from April 2027: are you in the £30,000 group?

If your qualifying income for the 2025–26 tax year was over £30,000, you need to use Making Tax Digital for Income Tax from 6 April 2027 (GOV.UK).

What counts towards £30,000

Self-employment turnover plus property income, before expenses. For a jointly owned property, only your share. Wages, pensions, dividends and partnership profit shares don't count (GOV.UK).

Example: £18,000 of freelance income and £14,000 of rent in 2025–26 gives £32,000. That's over £30,000, so MTD starts on 6 April 2027. Exactly £30,000 is not over the threshold.

Will HMRC tell me?

HMRC writes to people whose return shows income over the threshold. If no letter arrives, it is still your responsibility to check (GOV.UK).

What to do before 6 April 2027

  1. Confirm your 2025–26 qualifying income (it's on your 2025–26 return, due 31 January 2027).
  2. Choose software or a spreadsheet plus bridging software.
  3. Sign up. You can volunteer for the 2027–28 year now.
  4. Practise: keep this year's records digitally so April feels routine.
