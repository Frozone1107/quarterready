What counts as qualifying income for Making Tax Digital?

Whether and when you need Making Tax Digital for Income Tax depends on one figure: your qualifying income. It's your total income from self-employment and property, before expenses, taken from your Self Assessment return (GOV.UK).

What counts

  • Self-employment turnover (takings, fees, sales).
  • UK property income. If you're UK tax resident, foreign property income too.
  • For jointly owned property, only your share.

What doesn't count

  • Wages and salary from employment (PAYE).
  • State Pension and private pensions.
  • Dividends, including from your own company.
  • Your profit share as a partner in a partnership.

If your income drops later

Once you're using MTD, you can opt out if your qualifying income stays below the threshold for 3 tax years in a row (GOV.UK).
