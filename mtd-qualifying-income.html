MTD qualifying income: what counts and what doesn't

Short answer: your qualifying income for Making Tax Digital is your self-employment turnover plus your property income, before expenses, taken from your Self Assessment return. Salary, pensions and dividends don't count (GOV.UK).

What counts

  • Self-employment turnover (takings, fees, sales), before any expenses.
  • UK property income (rent), before expenses. If you're UK tax resident, foreign property income too.
  • For jointly owned property, only your share.

What doesn't count

  • Wages and salary from employment (PAYE).
  • State Pension and private pensions.
  • Dividends, including from your own company.
  • Your profit share as a partner in a partnership.

Common questions

Is MTD qualifying income before or after expenses?

Before. It's your gross turnover and gross rent. Expenses, mortgage interest and allowances don't reduce it.

Does my salary count towards MTD qualifying income?

No. Employment income taxed through PAYE doesn't count, so an employee with a small side business only counts the side business turnover.

Does VAT count?

Only if you use the cash basis and choose to include VAT in your turnover (GOV.UK).

What if my income drops below the threshold?

Once you're using MTD, you can opt out if your qualifying income stays below the threshold for 3 tax years in a row.
