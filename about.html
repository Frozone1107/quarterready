About QuarterReady

QuarterReady makes simple, one-time-purchase tools for UK sole traders and landlords moving to Making Tax Digital for Income Tax.

It is run by an independent developer based in France, [name]. We are not accountants and not affiliated with HMRC. That's why every rule we use links to the GOV.UK or HMRC page it comes from, with the date we last checked it.

How we keep it accurate

  • Rules are taken from GOV.UK guidance and HMRC's Making Tax Digital pages only.
  • We re-check them before each tax year and after each UK Budget, and update the tools.
  • If you spot something wrong, tell us and we'll correct it.

What we don't do

We don't submit anything to HMRC or give personal tax advice. The figures you type into the checker and the records you keep in the app stay in your browser; we never receive them.
