Consolidated expenses in MTD quarterly updates

If your annual turnover is under £90,000, you can send one total for all your expenses in a quarterly update instead of splitting it into HMRC's categories (QuickFile summary of HMRC's reporting categories).

Should you use it?

  • It's simpler each quarter: one figure for expenses.
  • A breakdown is still useful for you and your accountant at year end, and many people keep it anyway.

QuarterReady Records shows both: the breakdown per category and the consolidated total, so you can send either.

Landlords: what stays separate

Mortgage interest and other finance costs on residential lets are not deducted as an expense. They're recorded separately and give a basic-rate tax reduction when the year is finalised. Keep them out of your consolidated figure.

The categories, if you break it down

Self-employment: cost of goods, subcontractors, wages, car/van/travel, rent/rates/power/insurance, repairs, office costs, advertising, entertainment, loan interest, bank charges, bad debts, professional fees, depreciation, other.

UK property: rent/rates/insurance/ground rents, repairs and maintenance, non-residential finance costs, legal/management/professional fees, cost of services, travel, other allowable expenses.
