Help

Activating the full version

Your licence key is in the receipt email from our payment partner (search your inbox for "QuarterReady"). In the app, open Settings, paste the key and press Activate.

  • "Couldn't reach the licence server": check your internet connection and try again. If it keeps happening, email us your key.
  • New computer or browser: press "Remove from this browser" on the old one, then activate on the new one. Move your records with a backup file.
  • Lost your key: email us with the address you used to pay.

Your records

Records are saved in your browser on this device only. We never receive them, so we can't recover them for you.

  • Back up: Settings → Download backup. Keep the file with your business records.
  • Move to another device: download a backup, then on the other device use Settings → Restore from backup.
  • Private or incognito windows may not keep your records. Use a normal window.

Importing a bank statement

In online banking, download your statement as a CSV file (often under "Export" or "Download transactions"). In the app, open Bank import, choose the file and check the columns. Tick the business lines and pick a category for each. Next time, lines from the same payee are ticked and categorised for you. Lines already imported are skipped.

Receipts and mileage

Attach a photo or PDF when you add a record; it is stored in your browser with your records and included in backups. On the Mileage tab, log each business journey; the HMRC flat rate (55p a mile for the first 10,000 miles in a car or van from 2026–27, then 25p; motorcycles 24p) is added to "Car, van and travel expenses" automatically.

Sending your quarterly update

On the Quarterly summary tab, download the summary as CSV, open it in Excel or Google Sheets if needed, and link or import it in your HMRC-recognised bridging software. Check that the bridging software you choose accepts spreadsheet or CSV files.

Something isn't right
