    Questions

    Will HMRC tell me if I need to start?

    HMRC reviews your Self Assessment return and writes to you if your qualifying income is over the threshold. If no letter arrives, it's still your responsibility to check and sign up.

    Which year's income decides my start date?

    Over £50,000 in 2024–25 means 6 April 2026. Over £30,000 in 2025–26 means 6 April 2027. Over £20,000 in 2026–27 means 6 April 2028, according to current GOV.UK guidance.

    What if I'm under £20,000?

    You don't need MTD while your qualifying income stays at £20,000 or less. Check again each year.

    Does QuarterReady send my updates to HMRC?

    No. QuarterReady prepares the figures. To send them, use HMRC-recognised bridging software: copy in the totals or upload the CSV export, and it submits them for you.

    How do you keep up with rule changes?

    We check HMRC's guidance every month and after each Budget, and update the thresholds, deadlines and rates in the checker and the app. The date of the last check is shown above under "What's changed".

    Do quarterly updates replace my tax return?

    No. Quarterly updates are summaries of income and expenses. You still finish the year with a tax return from MTD software by 31 January after the tax year ends.

    Where are my records stored?

    Only in your browser, on your device. We never receive them. Download a backup from Settings regularly, because clearing your browser data deletes them.

    Can I put it on my phone like an app?

    Yes. Open the app, then use "Add to Home Screen" (iPhone, in Safari) or "Install app" (Android and computer, in Chrome or Edge). It gets its own icon and works offline.

    What happens after I buy?

    Our payment partner emails you a receipt with your licence key straight away. In the app, open Settings, paste the key and select Activate. The help page covers anything that goes wrong.

    Is this official?

    No. QuarterReady is independent and not affiliated with HMRC. It applies published rules to the figures you enter; your situation may involve rules it doesn't cover.
