How it works

You keep the records. We do the sums.

MTD has three jobs: record, add up, send. QuarterReady does the first two, which is where the time goes. The last step takes a few minutes in HMRC-recognised bridging software.

Done in QuarterReady
Bridging software
01 QuarterReady

Record

Type each income and expense, or import your bank statement and tick the business lines. Attach receipts, log business miles.

02 QuarterReady

Add up

Every line lands in the right HMRC category. You get year-to-date totals for each quarter, a consolidated expenses figure and duplicate warnings.

03 Bridging software

Send

Copy the totals, or upload the CSV export, into HMRC-recognised bridging software, which submits them. HMRC lists free options for simple affairs.

Why two tools? Only software recognised by HMRC may connect to its systems. QuarterReady is independent and isn't on that list, so it prepares your figures and leaves the sending to a recognised tool.

Questions

Good to know

Will HMRC tell me if I need to start?

HMRC reviews your Self Assessment return and writes to you if your qualifying income is over the threshold. If no letter arrives, it is still your responsibility to check and sign up.

Which year's income decides my start date?

Over £50,000 in 2024–25 means 6 April 2026. Over £30,000 in 2025–26 means 6 April 2027. Over £20,000 in 2026–27 means 6 April 2028, according to current GOV.UK guidance.

What if I'm under £20,000?

You're automatically exempt while your qualifying income stays at £20,000 or less. Check again each year.

Does QuarterReady send my updates to HMRC?

No. QuarterReady prepares the figures. To send them, you use HMRC-recognised bridging software: you copy in the totals or upload the CSV export, and it submits them for you.

Do quarterly updates replace my tax return?

No. Quarterly updates are summaries of income and expenses. You still finalise the year with a tax return from your MTD software by 31 January after the tax year ends.

Where are my records stored?

Only in your browser, on your device. We never receive them. Download a backup from Settings regularly, because clearing your browser data deletes them.

What happens after I buy?

Our payment partner emails you a receipt with your licence key straight away. In the app, open Settings, paste the key and press Activate. See the help page if anything goes wrong.

What's the difference between free and full?

Free keeps up to 30 records a tax year and shows your quarterly totals and deadlines. The full version (£19 once) removes the limit and adds bank statement import, receipt attachments, the mileage log, a per-property breakdown, CSV exports and a year-end report.

Is my bank statement uploaded anywhere?

No. The file is read inside your browser and never leaves your device. Only the lines you tick become records.

Is this official?

No. QuarterReady is independent and not affiliated with HMRC. It applies published rules to the figures you enter; your situation may involve rules it doesn't cover.
