Who is exempt from Making Tax Digital for Income Tax?

Exempt people keep filing a normal Self Assessment return. Some exemptions are automatic; others you must apply for (GOV.UK: exemptions).

Automatic, permanent (unless things change)

  • Qualifying income of £20,000 or less.
  • No National Insurance number before the tax year starts.
  • Trustees, personal representatives of someone who has died, and non-resident companies, for those returns.
  • Partnerships, for now.

Automatic until at least April 2027

If your 2024–25 return included averaging relief (farmers, creators), qualifying care relief (foster and kinship carers), or the SA107 trusts or SA109 residence pages. Ministers of religion, Lloyd's members and people claiming Married Couple's or Blind Person's Allowance have exemptions lasting beyond April 2027.

You must apply: digitally excluded

If your age, health or disability stops you using a computer or phone for records, your religious beliefs are incompatible with digital records, or you can't get internet access at home, work or elsewhere.

Not accepted on their own: having filed on paper before, being unfamiliar with software, having few records, or the time and cost of using MTD.
